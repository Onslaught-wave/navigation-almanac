Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Navigation Almanac app ("the App") handles your information. Your privacy matters to us, and the App is designed to keep your data on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not gather, store, or transmit any information about you to our servers or to anyone else — we operate no servers at all.

Location

The App can ask for access to your device's location. Location is used only on your device, solely to display your live position, course and speed in the GPS view and to pre-fill your observer position. Your location is never transmitted, shared, or stored off your device. Granting location access is optional — the rest of the App works without it, and you can enter your position manually.

Data you enter

Any values you enter (observer position, date/time, headings, etc.) and your settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
